# Integrated Frontend Update

The main MediSphere UI now includes a dedicated **Precision Care** screen at:

`#/precision-care`

It brings the second team's distinctive Milestone 4 frontend concepts into the main application UI:

- Patient 360 clinical snapshot
- Personalized AI-assisted careplan
- Care goals and interventions
- Outcome / CVD-risk trend visualization
- Care activity and adherence tracking
- Clinical guideline compliance and review
- Provider collaboration
- Care-team activity feed and messaging

The screen is demo-ready and self-contained so it does not require the second team's Spring Boot/PostgreSQL services just to demonstrate the UI.

The original second-team project remains preserved under the integrated project workspace for its backend/database/services.
